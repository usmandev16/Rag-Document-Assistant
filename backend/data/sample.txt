The Eiffel Tower is a wrought-iron lattice tower located on the Champ de Mars in Paris, France. It is named after the engineer Gustave Eiffel, whose company designed and built the tower.

The tower was constructed from 1887 to 1889 as the entrance to the 1889 World's Fair. Although initially criticized by some of France's leading artists and intellectuals for its design, it has become a global cultural icon of France.

The Eiffel Tower is the most-visited paid monument in the world. Millions of people ascend it every year. The tower is 330 metres tall, about the same height as an 81-storey building.
